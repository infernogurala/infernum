01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Interceptor handles information. In short: we do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information whatsoever.

Interceptor is designed to function strictly as a self-contained, offline utility on your Android device (similar to a native calculator, timer, or offline notepad). It requires no internet connectivity for core operation, requires no user sign-in or account creation, and contains zero background network reporting mechanisms.

02

Information We Collect: None

We strictly follow a zero-data-collection model:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, locations, or account credentials.
  • No User Accounts: There is no registration, login, or user profiling.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect or record your IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, advertising ID (AAID), or IP address.
  • No Usage Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or any other tracking tools to monitor how you use the app.
  • No Advertisements: The app contains zero ads and zero advertising SDKs.
Key Summary: Because Interceptor is an offline tool with no remote server backend, the developer has zero access to your data, your files, or your activity.
03

Device Storage & Permissions

To allow you to create, view, and organize your own local markdown notes and custom folders (such as local Obsidian vaults or document directories), Interceptor may request storage access:

Android Permission Why It Is Needed Data Handling
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Allows you to choose local folders on your device so the app can read and save your markdown notes (.md, .txt) directly on your device. 100% Local. Files are read and written strictly on your physical phone memory. No files are ever uploaded or transmitted outside your device.

Scope of Access: The app only interacts with the specific directories or markdown files you explicitly select. We never scan, harvest, or inspect your personal photos, videos, contacts, or files from other applications.

04

Data Storage, Retention, & Deletion

Where Your Data Resides: All your notes, settings, and feed preferences are saved exclusively in local storage on your device (in local app storage and your designated device folders).

How to Delete Your Data: You have absolute, direct control over all your data:

  • You can delete any note, card, or folder inside the app at any time.
  • You can clear all app preferences and data through your phone's Settings → Apps → Interceptor → Clear Data / Storage.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately removes the application and all its internal local cached files from your phone.

Because no data is ever transmitted to us or stored on any remote servers, there is no remote account or database record to delete.

05

Third-Party Services

Interceptor does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or remote tracking services. Font assets (such as bundled local fonts) are packaged directly with the application, ensuring that the app functions seamlessly in complete airplane/offline mode.

06

Children's Privacy

Because Interceptor is an offline tool that does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, it is safe for all audiences and does not collect any data from children or any other users under the age of 13.
